ByteStream - third-party software that ships next to bytestream.exe
====================================================================

go-librespot (go-librespot.exe)
  An open-source Spotify Connect player.  ByteStream starts it as a separate program and talks to it over a local
  HTTP API on 127.0.0.1; it is not linked into bytestream.exe.
  Licence:  GNU General Public License v3.0 (go-librespot-LICENSE.txt)
  Version:  see go-librespot-VERSION.txt (built unmodified from the upstream tag by .github/workflows/go-librespot.yml)
  Source:   https://github.com/devgianlu/go-librespot  (the tag named in go-librespot-VERSION.txt)

  It reaches Spotify through Spotify's own client protocol rather than a published API.  Spotify's terms do not
  permit that for third-party programs; ByteStream's Settings > Playback engine lets you choose Microsoft Edge
  (Spotify's official Web Playback SDK) instead.  Use it at your own risk, for your own Premium account.
